About Nepal DL Practice

Free driving license exam preparation for Nepal

Who We Are

Nepal DL Practice is a free, independent online platform designed to help Nepali citizens prepare for their driving license written exam (Likhit Pariksha). We provide 500+ MCQ practice questions covering Category A (Motorcycle) and Category B (Car/Vehicle) licenses.

Our Mission

We believe exam preparation should be free and accessible to everyone. Our goal is to help every Nepali driver pass their license exam on the first attempt by providing accurate, up-to-date practice questions in both English and Nepali.

What We Offer

  • 500+ real MCQ practice questions
  • Category A (Motorcycle) and Category B (Car) questions
  • Bilingual — English and Nepali
  • Exam mode with 30-minute timer
  • Instant results with explanations
  • Score history tracking
  • Traffic signs guide
  • Works offline after first visit
  • 100% free — no login required
