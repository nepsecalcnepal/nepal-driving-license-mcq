How to Apply for Driving License in Nepal 2026 न्यूनतम प्रक्रिया, कागजात, र शुल्क (Complete Step-by-Step Guide)

Published: June 2026 | Reading Time: 10 minutes

Applying for a driving license in Nepal has become much simpler in 2026. This complete guide will walk you through every step of the process, from eligibility to getting your license in hand.

1. Eligibility Requirements

Category A (Motorcycle)

  • Minimum age: 18 years
  • Nepali citizenship required
  • Basic reading and writing ability
  • Medical fitness certificate

Category B (Car/Vehicle)

  • Minimum age: 18 years
  • Nepali citizenship required
  • Must have Category A license for 1 year (for commercial)
  • Medical fitness certificate

2. Documents Required

  1. Citizenship Certificate (original + 2 photocopies)
  2. Passport-sized photos (4 recent photos, white background)
  3. Medical Certificate (from government hospital or registered doctor)
  4. Application Form (available at DOTM office)
  5. Fee receipt (after payment)

3. Step-by-Step Application Process

Step 1: Visit DOTM Office

Go to the Department of Transport Management (DOTM) office in your district. In Kathmandu, the main office is located at Tripureshwor.

Step 2: Get Application Form

Collect the driving license application form from the DOTM office. Fill it out completely with accurate information.

Step 3: Submit Documents

Submit the completed form along with all required documents (citizenship, photos, medical certificate).

Step 4: Pay Fee

Pay the application fee at the designated bank or counter inside DOTM office.

Step 5: Written Test (MCQ)

Take the written test consisting of 25 MCQ questions. You need 20+ correct answers (80%) to pass.

Step 6: Trial Test (Practical)

After passing the written test, you'll take a practical driving test. You need to demonstrate basic driving skills including:

  • Starting and stopping the vehicle
  • Turning and parking
  • Navigating through traffic
  • Understanding traffic signs

Step 7: Get Your License

After passing both tests, your license will be processed. You can collect it within 7-15 days from the DOTM office.

4. Fees (2026)

Service Fee (NPR)
Application Fee Rs. 500
License Fee (5 years) Rs. 1,500
Total Rs. 2,000

5. Timeline

  • Application to Written Test: 1-3 days
  • Written Test Result: Same day
  • Trial Test: 1-2 days after written test
  • License Issued: 7-15 days after passing
  • Total Time: Approximately 2-3 weeks

6. Tips to Pass the Written Test

  1. Practice MCQ Questions: Use our free practice test with 500+ questions
  2. Learn Traffic Signs: Memorize all red, yellow, and blue signs
  3. Understand Speed Limits: Know limits for different areas
  4. Read Question Carefully: Many questions are similar but have different answers
  5. Manage Time: 30 minutes for 25 questions = 1.2 minutes per question

FAQs

Where can I apply for driving license?

You can apply at DOTM (Department of Transport Management) offices in your district. Main office in Kathmandu is at Tripureshwor.

How long is the driving license valid?

Driving license is valid for 5 years. You can renew it before expiration.

What is the pass mark for written test?

You need 20+ correct answers out of 25 questions (80%) to pass the written test.
