Nepal Traffic Rules 2026: Complete Guide With Signs, Signals, Speed Limits & Fines

Published: June 2026 | Reading Time: 12 minutes

Understanding traffic rules is essential for safe driving and passing your driving license exam. This complete guide covers all Nepal traffic rules, signs, signals, speed limits, and fines for 2026.

1. Traffic Sign Categories

🔴 Mandatory Signs (Red Circular)

Red circular signs indicate prohibition - things you MUST NOT do:

  • Stop Sign - Must come to complete stop
  • No Entry - Do not enter this road
  • No Overtaking - Do not pass other vehicles
  • No Parking - Parking not allowed
  • Speed Limit - Maximum speed shown

🟡 Warning Signs (Yellow Triangle)

Yellow triangular signs warn about hazards ahead:

  • Curve Ahead - Sharp turn coming
  • Pedestrian Crossing - Watch for people
  • School Zone - Children crossing
  • Road Narrowing - Lane width decreases
  • Uneven Road - Bumpy road ahead

🔵 Informational Signs (Blue Rectangle)

Blue rectangular signs provide information:

  • Parking Area - Designated parking
  • Hospital - Medical facility nearby
  • One Way - Traffic flows one direction
  • Turn Direction - Left or right turn
  • Roundabout - Circular intersection

3. Traffic Signals

Red Light

STOP - Come to complete stop before the stop line. Do not proceed until light turns green.

Yellow Light

CAUTION - If you can stop safely, do so. If you're too close to stop safely, proceed with caution.

Green Light

GO - You may proceed, but check for pedestrians and other vehicles first.

4. Right of Way Rules

  • Emergency Vehicles: Always yield to ambulances, police, fire trucks
  • Pedestrians: Yield at crosswalks
  • Roundabouts: Vehicles already in roundabout have right of way
  • Intersection: Vehicle on right has priority (if no signals)
  • Hill Roads: Vehicle going uphill has priority

5. Prohibited Actions

  • ❌ Using mobile phone while driving
  • ❌ Not wearing helmet (motorcycle)
  • ❌ Not wearing seatbelt (car)
  • ❌ Overtaking on solid white line
  • ❌ Parking in no-parking zones
  • ❌ Driving under influence of alcohol
  • ❌ Exceeding speed limit
  • ❌ Running red lights
