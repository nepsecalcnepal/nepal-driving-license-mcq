Nepal Driving License Requirements & Fees 2026

Published: June 2026 | Reading Time: 8 minutes

Planning to get a driving license in Nepal? This complete guide covers all requirements, fees, categories, and processes for 2026.

Licence Categories

Category A - Motorcycle

  • Vehicles: Motorcycle, scooter, auto-rickshaw
  • Minimum Age: 18 years
  • Engine Capacity: Up to 250cc

Category B - Car

  • Vehicles: Car, jeep, van, small truck
  • Minimum Age: 18 years
  • Weight Limit: Up to 3,500 kg

Category C - Heavy Vehicle

  • Vehicles: Large trucks, buses
  • Minimum Age: 21 years
  • Requirement: Must have Category B for 1 year

Requirements

Age Requirements

Category A 18 years minimum
Category B 18 years minimum
Category C 21 years minimum

Documents Required

  1. Citizenship certificate (original + copies)
  2. 4 passport-sized photos (white background)
  3. Medical certificate
  4. Application form
  5. Fee payment receipt

Fees (2026)

Application Fee Rs. 500
License Fee (5 years) Rs. 1,500
Renewal Fee Rs. 1,000
International License Rs. 2,000
Learner's License Rs. 200

Renewal Process

When to renew: 30 days before expiration

Documents needed:

  • Old driving license
  • Citizenship certificate
  • 2 passport photos
  • Renewal fee
