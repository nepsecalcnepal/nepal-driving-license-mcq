Frequently Asked Questions

When is the driving license exam in Nepal?

Exams are conducted daily by DOTM in Kathmandu and weekly in other districts. Check dotm.gov.np for schedules.

How many questions are in the real exam?

The real exam has 25 MCQ questions with a 30-minute time limit. You need 20+ correct answers (80%) to pass.

What is the difference between Category A and B?

Category A is for motorcycles and scooters. Category B is for cars and light vehicles. Questions focus on the respective vehicle type.
